About

the 10-second story

Self-taught coder at fifteen. Co-founder of Hackowls Software at 22, acquired by QpiAI. Co-founder of Buyofuel since 2020, building the marketplace that moves India's farm waste from open fields into factory boilers.

Along the way, code gave way to marketing, and marketing gave way to markets. These days I run sales, partnerships and the narrative. Engineer by training, seller by trade!

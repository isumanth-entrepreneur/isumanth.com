About

I've spent the last decade building things end to end — first Hackowls Software, a services company I co-founded in 2017 that was acquired by QpiAI, and since 2020, Buyofuel, where I lead pan-India sales, partnerships and communications as co-founder.


Buyofuel connects agricultural and industrial waste to the furnaces and boilers of India's largest manufacturers — turning crop residue into energy, farm income and cleaner air. Self-taught from the w3schools era: code first, then marketing, then markets.

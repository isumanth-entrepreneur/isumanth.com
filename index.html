About

the 10-second story

I taught myself to code at fifteen. Building websites led to selling them, and that revealed the real craft: making people care about what's built. That craft became Hackowls Software, co-founded in 2017 and later acquired by QpiAI. Then in 2020 came Buyofuel, where farm waste that would have burned in open fields now fires the boilers of India's biggest manufacturers.

Code first, then marketing, then markets. These days I lead sales, partnerships and the story that holds it all together. Storyteller first, marketer second, engineer underneath.
